📖 THE STORY

Long ago the realm was one connected land, until a shattering spell tore it into a hundred drifting worlds, each guarded by a monster twisted by the magic that made it.

You are a Spellwalker — one of the rare few who can step between these worlds. Every monster you clear and every boss you defeat weakens the old barrier a little more, and every wizard you awaken along the way adds their power to yours.

Nobody knows what waits past World 100. Only Spellwalkers who have gone that far are starting to find out.
